# Surface brief: Zeno 64 TV screens (home guide, settings, Zeno's channel / screensaver)

Mode: Operate (home, settings) with an Experience channel (screensaver). Code-led, native Compose. Direction pinned by the owner; no concept roll.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The TV's home is a cable channel guide from a 1990s weather channel at dusk, with Zeno as the station mascot. It refuses the streaming-launcher default of rows of poster tiles and the AI-launcher default of glassy cards.
OWN-WORLD: Dusk sky gradient (indigo #1F1D6E to violet #5A3E8A to orange #E0893B), flat royal-blue panels (#2E3DA6) lit from the top-left with 3-4 dp light/dark bevels, yellow headings (#FFE14D), warm phosphor white (#F4F1E6), periwinkle secondary text (#B4BDF6), hard character-generator drop shadows, faint CRT scanlines, Jersey 15 pixel type, 12x12 pixel weather icons, Zeno's 32x32 sprite.
STORY: James sees the time, the weather and Zeno at a glance, moves one channel with the remote, presses OK, and is watching.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Header strip (Zeno badge box, "Zeno 64" + greeting in yellow, clock right), one bevelled panel split 500/rest: numbered channel list left (focused row lit panel + pixel arrow + yellow), Zeno at 4x with a line of personality and the weather right; yellow-labelled ticker full width at the bottom.
FORM: Pinned by the owner (weather-channel information screen); the concept roll was waived because the owner pinned the direction, so no seed exists. seed: none (waived, owner-pinned)
SIGNATURE: Zeno glances at the row you move to, then blinks back; the channel cycles pages with hard cuts every 10 s like the original.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
